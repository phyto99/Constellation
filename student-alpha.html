◈ Alpha Application
The system has seen enough of your play to know you understand what's happening.
This application asks you to demonstrate that understanding explicitly.

Reference a specific session or series of sessions you played. Write in plain language. The system is not looking for correct terminology — it is looking for evidence that you understand what the session was training.
Phase 1
Pre-Session Planning
What did you observe about your team's approach before the session started? Did they appear to have a shared plan? What strategy does the opening seem to suggest?
Phase 2
Round by Round
Walk through the session as a detective. What decisions were made? Where did the plan hold and where did it break down? You are reading what decisions reveal about the team's model — not narrating the game.
Phase 3
Team Dynamics
How effectively did the team coordinate? Who drove decisions? Were there moments where individual play diverged from team strategy? What did this cost?
Phase 4
Detective Observations
What did you notice that the players probably did not notice themselves? A pattern across rounds. A resource consistently left unused. A decision that made sense locally but cost them at the session level. Something specific and verifiable.
Phase 5
Course Correction
If you could pause the session at the end of round 3 and say one thing — not after, during — what would it be? Why round 3? What did the data show by then that would have changed something if acted on?
All five phases are required.
You can only have one pending application at a time.
