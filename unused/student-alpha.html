CONSTELLATION — ALPHA APPLICATION Submit a constructive analysis of a team's Constellation session. Your analysis is evaluated not on whether the team won or lost, but on the quality of your observations. Alpha status is awarded for demonstrated understanding, not for performance.
Session Reference
Which session are you analyzing?
Phase 1 — Before the Session
If you observed the planning stage: What was the team's apparent strategy going in? Did they have a shared understanding of what the session required? What did their preparation reveal?
If no planning stage was visible, note what the team's opening moves suggest about their pre-game thinking.
Phase 2 — The Playing Stage
Walk through the session as a detective. What did the team do in each round? Where did decisions align with the session's requirements, and where did they not? Be specific — name rounds, name players if possible, name the decision and why you read it as you do.
You are not narrating the game. You are reading what the decisions reveal about the team's model of the session.
Phase 3 — Team Coordination
How effectively did the team work together? Who drove the key decisions? Were there moments where individual play diverged from team strategy? What was the effect?
Phase 4 — Detective Observations
What did you notice that the team probably didn't notice themselves? A pattern across rounds. A resource consistently left unused. A decision that made sense locally but cost them at the session level. Something specific and verifiable — not general advice.
This is the most important part of your application. The more specific the better — "their steal rate dropped to zero after round 5, even though their opponent's network was most vulnerable in rounds 6 and 7" is better than "they didn't steal enough."
Phase 5 — Course Correction
If you could pause the session at the end of round 3 and say one thing to the team — not after, during — what would it be? Why round 3? What did you know by then that they apparently didn't?
Round 3 is significant: early enough to change the session, late enough to have real data.
Anything Else
Anything your analysis didn't capture. Questions you had. Things you noticed but couldn't fully explain.
Your application will be reviewed by the class administrator and, if other Alphas exist, by them as well. You will receive a response — not a score, but a reaction. There is no penalty for applying and being declined.
