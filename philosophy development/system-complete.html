Per-Mode Information Landscapes

Why the peak is at 30–40th pct, not 50th

At 50th percentile the student won comfortably. The outcome was somewhat expected — the posterior update is moderate. At 30–40th percentile the student barely won. The outcome was genuinely uncertain — maximum surprise while still being processable. This is where the information gain function peaks. The asymmetry (overwhelm kills faster than boredom) means target the lower edge, not the center.

The forgetting decay adjustment

Returning to a mode after τ days: d_adjusted = d_base × exp(−τ/λ) where λ ≈ 14 days (to be learned from data). Without this, a student returning to SPATIAL after 3 weeks receives a difficulty calibrated for someone who played yesterday. The information gain curve shifts left with time away — the ZPD shrinks as skills decay. The system must account for this or risk pushing students past their current ZPD.

Velocity bonus in mode selection

Two students at Elo 500 in SPATIAL. One has been improving at +30 Elo/session; the other is flat for 6 sessions. The improving student is in an active learning arc — pulling them away for coverage costs more than the benefit. The plateau student needs a mode change or template change — staying in SPATIAL is not working. The velocity term in the selection weight captures this: students in active improvement arcs should be allowed to continue; plateaued students should be rotated.

The final state

The student who has converged does not have a strategy — they have a model of the game sophisticated enough to generate the correct strategy for any configuration on the fly. This is maximum entropy output: their behavior is unpredictable from prior sessions because it is fully conditioned on the current moment, not habit. INVERSION stops being an overlay and becomes their permanent operating mode. The system has nothing left to teach. That is the objective.

Terminal System — The Student Experience

The terminal is the student's only window into the system's reasoning. It is calibrated for optimal obscurity: enough signal that the student knows the system is watching something real, not enough to tell them what to do next. The messages create a meta-community — students compare what their terminal said and theorize about what patterns the system is tracking. No telltale signs, but never pure noise.

The Calibration Principle

Too revealing — never use

"You should connect your HQs faster."
"Claim stars near the center first."
"Your distance score was 47% below optimal."
"Use blackholes more aggressively."

These tell the student what to do. The system should observe, not instruct. Instruction removes the cognitive work that produces development.

Optimally calibrated — use these

"HQ network collapsed in round 4. Logged."
"Three direction changes in the opening sequence."
"Steal rate: 0. The mechanism was available."
"This pattern has been observed before."

Observational, factual, verifiable. Creates the question "what does that mean?" without a one-word answer. Forces reflection, not imitation.

Too obscure — avoid

"Signal vector 7 deviated by σ=2.3."
"Pattern 7-δ detected in behavioral cluster."
"Entropy threshold exceeded."
"Coefficient matrix adjusted."

Meaningless to the student. Creates no discussion, no reflection. The community cannot theorize about signals they cannot connect to anything they did.

Message Library — Complete Calibrated Message System

Messages are organized by register. Behavioral observation: what the system actually saw. Calibration: what is changing and why, without revealing how. Recognition: acknowledging achievement without explaining significance. Overlay: controlled epistemic disruption. Meta-community: connecting the student to a history that predates them.

The surveillance aesthetic — calibrated

The terminal should feel like it knows more about the session than the student does. Not because it is mysterious, but because it is measuring things the student was not thinking to track.

"You noticed you lost. The system noticed you lost in round 3 of 10, that your HQ network was 40% shorter than your best, and that your steal rate dropped to zero in the second half."

The student sees the rank. The terminal sees the signals. The gap between those two perspectives is the productive mystery.
The anti-pattern. Messages that reference abstract system internals ("entropy budget adjusted") are not surveillance — they are noise. Surveillance messages must reference something the student actually did in a form they can recognize. The system is observing the student, not reporting on its own state.
